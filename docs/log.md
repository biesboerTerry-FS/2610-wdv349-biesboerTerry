### Week 1

## Hi everyone

For my project, I will be redesigning and rebuilding the website for my daughter's former high school ultimate frisbee team.
My primary motivation for this project comes directly from my experience while she was playing. Keeping everyone on the same page was often a significant challenge. To be successful, this website needs to serve three distinct audiences, each with very different priorities:

* **The Players: Who need quick access to practice schedules, game locations, and team stats.**

* **The Parents: Who need clear logistical information, volunteer sign-up sheets, and contact forms.**

* **The Coaching Staff: Who need a reliable, centralized platform to post immediate announcements and manage team rosters.**

Currently, information is scattered, and I want to build a unified, easy to navigate hub that solves these communication gaps and provides a better user experience for the whole community.

For my tech stack, I will be using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, and Node.js). Even though I am building a dynamic application, I plan to keep the page architecture straightforward. Because reliability is crucial for a community hub, I plan to integrate automated Selenium functional tests against the critical components, like the homepage and contact forms.

This project will be a massive help to my overall portfolio because it demonstrates my ability to build a full stack application that solves a real world problem. It also directly supports my future career aspirations by highlighting my focus on practical application testing and reliable frontend design.

I am really looking forward to building this, and I am excited to see what everyone else is working on!
